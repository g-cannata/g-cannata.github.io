# About

I am a doctoral researcher in _Transational Governance_ – yes, I myself have been struggling to figure out what it means. More practically, I do research on public policy and would go as far as describing myself as a policy analyst. I have been doing research on quite a few topics in the scholarly field of 'EU stuff', and I have also worked hands-on at the science-policy interface, especially on projects concerning knowledge brokerage and science-for-policy initiatives.

I have spent my adult life so far wrapping my head around policymaking and how it occurs – in all fairness, with rather modest results – and I feel like it has not been time well spent.

About me, I like Pina Coladas and getting caught in the rain, but what I am really keen on is sarcasm. I am fluent in cynicism and and I guess wordplay in titles are the only salvageable part of my academic work. In my research, I have explored different approaches and methods applied to the study energy and climate policy, migration governance, and the role(s) of knowledge and evidence in policymaking, with a particular focus on the European Union context.
